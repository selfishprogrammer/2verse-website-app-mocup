Healing is not a transaction. It is a return, to your breath, your body, and the quiet place beneath the noise.

Trust & verification

Invite-only. Quality over quantity.

2Verse is gated on purpose. Healers join by invitation or application and are verified for each modality before they appear publicly. We never push you toward one healer. You choose based on credentials, story and real reviews.

Identity verified

Government ID is checked before any profile goes live.

Credentials, per modality

Certificates, training hours and expiry dates are reviewed separately for each modality.

Lineage & training

The guru, school or tradition each healer trained in is recorded and checked.

Honest reviews

Only clients who attended a session can leave a review, and healers can't remove them.
